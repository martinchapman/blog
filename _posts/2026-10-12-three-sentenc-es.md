A colleague used to adhere to the [three.sentenc.es](http://three.sentenc.es/) policy when writing email.
Although this policy can harm tone, the general theme of brevity that it feeds into is an important one.

I regularly apply a similar approach to technical problems, often a very straightforward one: how do I name a particular file?
By always prioritising brevity, a file that may have been called `aws_model_inference_and_authentication.py` (difficult to parse) simply ends up being `aws.py` (easy to parse), with the additional information in the title communicated elsewhere.
The same approach can be applied to text in any digital setting, including blog posts.
